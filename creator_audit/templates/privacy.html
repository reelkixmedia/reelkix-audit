YouTube API Services. This site uses YouTube API Services to read public channel and video data. By using it you are also bound by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access through the Google security settings page if you ever connect an account.

What we collect. Your email, your channel link, the YouTube Studio export you upload, and (Premium) your goals form. For outreach teasers we use only public YouTube data plus an email address the creator published on their own website or link-in-bio page.

What we keep. Uploaded CSV files are deleted as soon as your report is built; we keep only the computed metrics, your score and the report text. Public YouTube API data used for outreach is refreshed or deleted after {{ cfg.API_DATA_RETENTION_DAYS }} days.

Your choices. Delete your data at any time, or email {{ cfg.SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. Every outreach email has a one-click unsubscribe, after which we never contact that address or channel again.

Processors. Stripe (payments), Anthropic (report writing — receives computed metrics, not your raw file), our email provider.
